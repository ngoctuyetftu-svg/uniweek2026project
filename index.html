06 · Learning Points

Challenges & takeaways

What got in the way, how I handled it, and what I will do differently as a Project Lead.

Challenge

Compressed timeline

Holiday periods and event dates squeezed the preparation window, including POSM production.

What I did

Finalized the schedule early, assigned clear ownership and KPIs per workstream, ran regular PM and team check-ins, and built a handbook so every tour PM followed the same playbook.

Outcome

100% of planned scope delivered: 11 tours across 13 universities.

Challenge

Consistent quality across 11 tours

Different venues and teams meant risks in setup, group management and presentation playback.

What I did

Standardized the onsite setup with a dedicated tech laptop, introduced tour guide flags, and optimized tea-break planning.

Outcome

Shorter setup time, groups stayed together on campus, and the Operations score improved versus Nov 2025.

Challenge

New partners and BU engagement

Limited lead time to plan BU involvement, plus longer coordination with first-time university partners.

What I did

Extended coordination with new partners, aligned BU speakers to each university's majors, and brought Zalopay and VNGGames in as gift sponsors.

Outcome

3 new university partners joined. Speaker satisfaction reached 4.91/5, with 94% willing to return.

Challenge

Session balance

Students felt industry sharing ran long while Q&A was too short.

What I did

Collected structured feedback from every tour (90% response rate) to pinpoint the issue with data instead of impressions.

Next step

Rebalance interaction time in the agenda and give speakers clearer context.

Key takeaways for my next project
01

Plan by milestones

Define milestone deliverables and run one centralized kick-off. Extend the preparation window by 2 to 3 weeks.

02

Standardize to scale

Handbooks, setup standards and technical testing let a program grow without losing quality.

03

Involve stakeholders early

Bring HR, business units and creative into reviews sooner, and keep buffer time for new partners.

04

Let data steer decisions

Satisfaction, NPS and response rate show what to improve. Share live updates with core stakeholders.
